Understanding the Metrics

High Upside

This represents the maximum peak the index reached during the period, measured from its opening price. It tells you the highest potential profit that was available if you had caught the top.

Low Drawdown

This represents the maximum dip the index suffered during the period, measured from its opening price. It tells you the maximum "pain" or risk the index went through during that time.
